# Warning workflow

**Status:** Direct Admin management, reversible deletion, and Negative Point-linked Automatic Warnings are shipped in repository `main` at [`fcf33a57cfe6b2573ee224889c73e7383a5d20fe`](https://github.com/ehuerta6/cappy-hub/tree/fcf33a57cfe6b2573ee224889c73e7383a5d20fe); production deployment is not established here. Legacy approval records and decisions are historical data, not the normal current workflow.

**Actors:** Admins create, correct, Delete/Restore, and review Warnings; Officers see eligible Warnings on their own profiles; historical approvers remain relevant only for legacy records.

Admins create effective Warnings and may correct reasons. New records do not wait for leadership votes. Older pending/rejected statuses and approval assignments remain visible where needed for historical compatibility; an edit does not convert those records into approved Warnings. Current Warnings can be reversibly deleted/restored; this lifecycle is distinct from the older terminal Void action and preserves provenance. The approved sources are [Warnings](../../docs/product/warnings.md), [Administration and audit history](../../docs/product/administration-audit.md), and decisions for [#335 and #336](../../docs/product/planned-accepted.md).

Groups of three active Negative Points can create an Automatic Warning linked to those transactions. Removing the linked Warning removes its linked Negative Points; removing a linked Negative Point removes the Warning while preserving the other transactions. Restoring an Automatic Warning restores its linked Negative Points only if none have since been linked to another Warning. At the three-Warning limit, further Automatic or Manual Warnings are not created until a slot is freed, but Negative Points remain recordable. Restore of a deleted approved, non-voided Warning is rejected while the Officer already has three active Warnings; restoring one at two active Warnings makes it the third and triggers the leadership notice defined in [Notifications](notifications.md). Manual Warnings do not change Points. Warnings connect to Officer profiles and review decisions but do not control Officer access automatically. Three active Warnings flag the Officer for Admin review; they do not deactivate the Officer. Keep warning counts, Officer lifecycle and account authorization distinct (see [Officers](officers.md) and [Identity and access](identity-access.md)).
