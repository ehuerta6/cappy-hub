# Warnings

## Warnings

### Current / shipped

Admins create immediately effective Warnings with a nonblank reason; no leadership approval is required. Admins can correct the reason on current or historical records, including voided Warnings. Corrections preserve the recipient, original creation time, status, lifecycle and historical approval rows, and record meaningful before/after reasons with actor attribution in audit history. An unrelated edit never approves a historical pending or rejected Warning.

Under [Issue #402](https://github.com/ehuerta6/cappy-hub/issues/402), an Officer may have at most three active Warnings total. Active means approved, not voided, and not deleted. Admin-created Warnings are Manual and never change Points. Every group of three active Negative Points can create one Automatic Warning, linked to exactly those three transactions. Removing a linked negative Point removes that Automatic Warning but leaves the other two negative transactions active and available to form a later group. Removing an Automatic Warning removes its three linked negative transactions; removing a Manual Warning does not change Points. Both origins use the existing Admin edit and reversible Delete/Restore workflow, and the origin is retained when a reason is corrected. Restoring an Automatic Warning also restores its linked negative Points if they have not since been linked to another Warning. Restore of a deleted approved, non-voided Warning is rejected while the Officer already has three active Warnings; restoring one at two active Warnings makes it the third and triggers the in-app leadership notice defined in [Notifications](notifications.md). At the three-Warning limit, negative Points remain recordable, but no further Automatic or Manual Warning is created until a slot is freed.

Historical pending and rejected Warnings remain visible to Admins; the read-only legacy assignment view preserves assigned approver visibility. The existing trusted decision RPC remains compatible with explicitly deciding a legacy pending snapshot; new Warnings have no approval rows and cannot receive votes. Officers see approved, non-voided Warnings on their own profile. Only active approved Warnings count toward the threshold. Three active approved Warnings flag the Officer for Admin review; they do not automatically deactivate the Officer. Admins use reversible Delete/Restore to remove current Warnings from active totals and recover them without changing their prior eligible state. Restore does not approve a historical pending or rejected Warning. Existing voided Warnings retain their original status, approvals, decisions, timestamps, and void attribution; Void remains terminal for those historical records, which cannot receive further decisions. Admins can inspect Voided or All history. Existing Void actions are audited, and historical `warning.deleted` entries remain unchanged.
